# Privacy policy

This is the default policy of an MKWMP server. The person who hosts this server runs it, not the game's publisher
and not the MKWMP project, and should replace this text with their own (`[privacy] file` in the
server's config).

## Who can use it

Only people 18 or older.

## What the server keeps

In its database: your **gamertag**, a **hash of your PIN** (the PIN itself is never stored), when you
registered and last used it, when you accepted this policy, your **friends and friend requests**, and
any **voice-chat or camera report** you file (who you reported, why, what you wrote, when, and the
host's note once it is reviewed). It doesn't ask for your email, real name, age or payment details.

## What it only holds while it runs

Seats and the queue, who is online, the game lobbies, and rate-limit counters keyed by your IP address.
They are in memory and gone when the server restarts.

## Logs

The server's logs can contain your gamertag, the game's user id and your IP address. The host uses them
to fix problems and look into reports.

## Voice chat and camera

Voice and camera pictures are relayed live to the players in your friend room and are never recorded or
stored by the server.

## Who sees what

Your friends see whether you're playing, online or offline. Players in your lobby see your gamertag. The
game connects the players in a lobby to each other, so they may see your IP address, as in any online
game. Only the host sees reports.

## How long it is kept

A gamertag unused for the number of days the host set (30 by default) is deleted, with its friends and
requests. Reports are deleted 90 days after they are filed.

## Your choices

Deleting your account in the launcher removes your gamertag, PIN hash, friends and the reports you filed.
You can withdraw your consent to this policy in the launcher; the server may then stop giving you a seat.

## Changes

When this text changes, the launcher asks you to accept it again.
